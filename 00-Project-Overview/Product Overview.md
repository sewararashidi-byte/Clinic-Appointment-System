Clinic Appointment System

Product Description
The Clinic Appointment System is a software system developed to make the process of scheduling and managing clinic appointments easier and more organized. 
It provides patients with a central place where they can search for doctors, check their availability, and book, cancel, or reschedule appointments.
Doctors can use the system to manage their availability and keep track of their scheduled appointments. Receptionists or administrators can manage patient information, 
doctor information, and appointment records.
The system is intended to reduce the need for manual appointment scheduling and record-keeping. By keeping the information in one place, it can help the clinic manage 
appointments more efficiently and make the process more convenient for patients, doctors, and staff.

Product Vision
The vision of the Clinic Appointment System is to provide a reliable and easy-to-use system that makes appointment scheduling more convenient for patients while helping 
doctors and clinic staff manage appointments, schedules, and related information efficiently.

Product Objectives
The main objectives of the Clinic Appointment System are to:
	Provide patients with an easy way to search for doctors and available appointment slots.
	Allow patients to book, cancel, and reschedule their appointments.
	Allow doctors to manage their availability and view their scheduled appointments.
	Allow receptionists or administrators to manage patient, doctor, and appointment information.
	Reduce manual work involved in appointment scheduling and record-keeping.
	Prevent appointment conflicts and double-booking.
	Keep appointment information organized and up to date.
	Improve communication and coordination between patients, doctors, and clinic staff.
	Provide a centralized system for managing clinic appointments.
